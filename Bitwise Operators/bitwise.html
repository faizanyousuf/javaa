What are bitwise Operators?

Bitwise operators work directly on the individual bits of the integer

Main Bitwise Operators

Operator Name Example
& Bitwise AND 5 & 8
| Bitwise OR 5 | 9
^ Bitwise Xor 5 ^ 2
~ Bitwise Not ~4
>> Right shift 5>>1

important Properties

  • Remove last set bit

    • n = n & (n-1)

  • Get last set bit

    • n & (-n)
